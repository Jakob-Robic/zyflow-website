# Delete your Zyflow account

Slovenščina: [https://www.zyflow.eu/legal-pages/izbris-racuna](https://www.zyflow.eu/legal-pages/izbris-racuna)

This page explains how to delete your account in the Zyflow app (developer: HERKO d.o.o., Kranj, Slovenia) and what happens to your data.

## Option 1 – in the app (fastest)

1. Open Zyflow and sign in.
2. Tap the **Profile** tab, then the **gear icon** (Settings) at the top right.
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account** (below Log Out).
4. Confirm with **Yes, delete my account**.

Your account and profile data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

## Option 2 – by email (if you cannot use the app)

Write to [legal@zyflow.eu](mailto:legal@zyflow.eu) with the subject "Delete my account", from the email address linked to your Zyflow account. To protect your account, we only delete it after we have verified that the request comes from the account holder: if you write from that address, we will ask you to confirm by replying; if you cannot write from that address (for example you used Sign in with Apple with a hidden email), we will send a confirmation to the email address linked to the account and delete it once you confirm. We will never ask for your password. We confirm the deletion by email.

## Delete some of your data and keep your account

You don't have to delete your account to remove part of your data.

**In the app:**

- **Routes:** open a saved or imported route and tap **Delete route**. A community route you saved from someone else is removed with **Remove from My routes**.
- **Routes you shared with the community:** open the route and tap **Delete route**. Copies that other riders saved before you deleted it stay in their accounts.
- **Saved places:** tap the saved icon on a place, or **Remove saved place**.
- **Photos:** remove your profile photo with **Remove photo** in your profile; delete a bike photo by tapping it.
- **Bike passport:** clear or change any field (for example weight, frame number, value or notes) and save.
- **Strava and other connected services:** tap **Disconnect**. Disconnecting Strava also deletes the rides and routes imported from Strava. Stamps you already earned stay in your stamp book; ask us by email if you want them removed.
- **Recent searches:** remove them from the search list on your phone.
- **Emails from Zyflow:** use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter email and we stop sending them.

**By email:** for anything you cannot delete in the app (for example stamps, place reports, analytics records or error reports), write to [legal@zyflow.eu](mailto:legal@zyflow.eu) with the subject "Delete some of my data" and tell us what you want removed. Write from the email address linked to your account; we verify the request the same way as for account deletion. We delete the data **within 30 days** and confirm by email. We may keep data we must keep by law, or data without which your account cannot work (your email address and user ID).

## What we delete

Deleting your account removes your profile (name, email address, phone number, user ID, sign-in details) and the data stored with your account in our database: saved and imported routes, saved places, routes you shared with the community, your bike passport, and your place reports and submissions.

Deleting your account also automatically removes your photos and map images from our file storage, your contact at our email provider (Brevo) and your analytics records (PostHog). Error reports linked to your user ID (Sentry) are deleted automatically within 90 days at the latest. **If you want them removed sooner, write to [legal@zyflow.eu](mailto:legal@zyflow.eu) after deleting your account and we will remove them within 30 days.**

## What we keep, and for how long

- Places you suggested that we approved stay in the Zyflow places database, without any link to you.
- Copies of a shared route that other users saved to their own accounts before you deleted yours may remain in their accounts.
- We currently keep no separate database backups, so deleted data does not remain in backups.
- Technical server and hosting logs (which can contain IP addresses) are kept for up to 30 days.
- Search rate-limit records (IP address and counters) are kept only as long as needed to prevent abuse and are regularly deleted.
- Email sending logs at Brevo are kept only as long as needed to deliver emails and resolve delivery problems, in line with the provider's retention settings.
- If you wrote to us, we keep our correspondence as long as needed to handle your request and, afterwards, for a limited period to document it and to defend legal claims; accounting records are kept as required by law.
- Data stored on your phone (login session, last location, recent searches) is removed when you uninstall the app.

## How long it takes

Your account, profile data, files, Brevo contact and analytics records are deleted immediately when you delete your account in the app. Requests sent by email, and early removal of error reports, are completed **within 30 days** of your request. More about how we handle your data: [Privacy Policy](https://www.zyflow.eu/legal-pages/privacy-policy). Questions: [legal@zyflow.eu](mailto:legal@zyflow.eu).
