It started with a long climb, a flat battery, and a better plan.

We’re the small team behind Zyflow — building smart navigation for cyclists and e-bike riders who want to go further without guessing their range. Today we map chargers across Europe and keep expanding the continent and country route library. We’ve never lost sight of what that first hard ride taught us: elevation, weather, and charging stops belong in the plan before you leave.
