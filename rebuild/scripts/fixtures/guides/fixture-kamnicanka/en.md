## Where the climbing is

The route runs from Kranj through Šenčur, Adergas and Cerklje to Kamnik and
back. 254 m over 70 km is about 3.6 m of climbing per kilometre, so you rarely
need your lowest gear.

## Where to stop

Arboretum Volčji Potok, Strmol Castle and Slovenia's only bike bridge across a
motorway are all on the loop. Each one is a natural break on a long day.

## Who it suits

Riders who are comfortable with 70 km in a day. On an e-bike, check before you
set off that your battery covers the full distance.
