Most rides that go wrong were decided before the start. The battery was planned for the flat, the wind turned on the way home, or the last café with a socket was closed on Mondays. Riding smarter is a set of small planning habits, not a fitter body or a bigger battery.

## What this guide covers

These posts are not about one place. They are the skills that work on any ride, in Gorenjska or across a border: reading range honestly, reading an elevation profile, planning stops and chargers, and checking the weather where it matters.

## How to use it

Start with the battery-aware day ride if you are new to e-bike planning. Read the elevation guide before your first ride with a big climb or a border on it. Every guide ends with the one habit in Zyflow that does the work for you.

## The short version

- **Plan range the way you really ride.** Not the way the brochure rides
- **Find the tight section.** The hardest climb with the furthest charger is the part to plan around
- **Put a stop in the plan.** Even if you never use it
- **Check the wind for the way home.** It can cost more than the climb
